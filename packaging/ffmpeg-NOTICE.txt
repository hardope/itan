FFmpeg
======

Itan's desktop apps include ffmpeg and ffprobe from FFmpeg 9.0.2, which
Itan runs to read videos, make thumbnails and convert videos browsers
can't play. They are separate programs, not part of Itan's code.

These builds include GPL-licensed parts (such as x264 and x265), so they
are licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Its text is
in GPL-3.0.txt next to this file, and at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt

Source code: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
A copy is also attached to every Itan release that includes these builds:
https://github.com/hardope/itan/releases

The builds come from:
- macOS: Martin Riedl, https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de
  (build scripts: https://git.martin-riedl.de/ffmpeg/build-script)
- Windows: Gyan Doshi, https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
  (the build's README lists its configuration and the libraries in it)
